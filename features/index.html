Features

Everything Cedar does, on every screen.

Cedar brings authorized media sources, live channels, personal libraries, and watch progress into one private, local-first player. Here’s what it can do, and where.

Android TV · Google TV · Fire TV · iPhone · iPad · Apple TV · Mac

Get Cedar What’s new

What’s in Cedar

Some features depend on the device, and on what your sources and servers provide.

Home

Branches and the hero

  • Branches, your way

    Home is built from branches you control. Add, rename, reorder, hide or restore them, and choose poster, wide or hero rows.

  • The hero

    Choose the hero’s style and details, which rating sources it shows, and whether a trailer starts after a moment.

  • Ready-made Homes

    Browse curated setups and branch groups in Cedar Creator, add a profile photo, and open them in Cedar.

    Open Cedar Creator

Live TV

Guide, catch-up, DVR

  • The guide

    Playlist order, XMLTV programme data, a focused grid guide, and an in-guide preview player keep channels fast to browse.

  • Catch-up

    Play programmes that have already aired, within the window your provider keeps an archive for.

  • Multiview and DVR

    Jellyfin and Emby Live TV join Live TV on Apple devices, with lineups, multiview and Cedar DVR.

    About Cedar Server

  • Customize Live

    Arrange the Live page as shelves on iPhone, with programme pages that show where a show airs.

Playback

Picture and sound

  • Your languages

    System-language audio, subtitles off by default, and preferred audio and subtitle languages, including forced and SDH tracks.

  • Skip Intro and Up Next

    Skip intros where segment data exists, with an optional Up Next card and countdown before the next episode.

  • Dolby Vision

    Optionally convert supported Dolby Vision Profile 7 material to Profile 8.1 as it plays.

Discover

Search and picks

  • What Should I Watch

    Ranked picks in Search on Apple devices, which you can save as a Home row.

  • Top 10 Charts

    Top 10 Charts and public MDBList lists, right on Home.

  • Sports Bar

    Scores on Home, with as much detail as each competition’s data allows.

Sources

Servers and shares

  • Plex, Emby and Jellyfin

    Connect your media servers and browse their movies, series, seasons and episodes with their own artwork.

  • WebDAV shares

    Browse and play folders you’re authorized to use, with movie and series rows built from file names.

  • Playlists and guides

    Add a remote M3U playlist and XMLTV guide, or pick local files on iPhone and iPad.

    Open browser setup

Extras

And the rest

  • Cedar Radio

    Radio stations by country and genre, with synced lyrics and Shazam.

    About Cedar Radio

  • .cedar files

    Share and import .cedar setup files, with passphrase-locked passwords.

    Open Cedar Creator

  • Mark Segments

    Send intro, recap and credits times to IntroDB and SkipDB from Apple devices.

Made for each screen

Cedar keeps one product language, then lets each device do what it does best.

Downloads

Save supported movies and episodes for later, with a Download Center to manage them, on iPhone and iPad.

Picture in Picture and AirPlay

On iPhone and iPad, keep watching in the corner or send it to another screen, when the stream supports it.

Top Shelf and Watch Next

Pick up where you left off from Apple TV’s Top Shelf, or the Watch Next row on Android TV where it’s supported.

Radio in CarPlay

Cedar Radio stations come along in the car, on iPhone.

A profile for everyone

Separate profiles at home, and Kids profiles with the content-rating limit you choose.

Every Apple device in step

Profiles, favorites, lists and progress sync through your own iCloud. Passwords stay out of ordinary sync.

Your media. Your devices. Your control.

Private by design

Bring authorized sources

Connect media servers, playlists, and services you own, operate, or have permission to use. Cedar ships with no media, subscriptions, or provider credentials.

Keep credentials protected

Provider passwords, tokens, and API keys are kept in the system Keychain rather than ordinary preferences. Cedar has no required developer-operated user account.

Choose what syncs

Use Cedar entirely on-device, or opt into private Apple sync. Cedar Household adds an optional encrypted way to share selected profile data.

No tracking or ads

Cedar has no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated analytics backend. Services you deliberately connect receive only the requests needed for the features you enable.

Straightforward policies

Cedar supplies the player, not the content.

Connect only sources and services you are authorized to use. The public privacy, content, and accessibility pages explain how the app behaves before you install it.
