      READY FOR CEDAR

      Your Home, ready to take with you.

      1. Download your collection JSON.
      2. In Cedar on iPhone, iPad, or Mac, open Settings → Home Screen & Branches → Import Collection JSON.
      3. Review the import in Cedar. Your configured sources supply the titles.

      Direct Branches import as poster rows. Branch Group device layouts use Cedar’s Studio layout support. This preview does not send changes directly to a device.
