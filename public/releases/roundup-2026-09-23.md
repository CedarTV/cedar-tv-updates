# Cedar cross-platform changelog roundup

September 23, 2026

## Key features since external build 41

- Sports Bar in Library: follow leagues and teams, browse past and upcoming games around Today, and open scores, available statistics, standings and rosters. Game selection looks for matching programmes in your connected guides.
- Quick Actions branches: add Random Episode, Random Movie, Random Show, Recently Added and Recently Watched tiles, with optional Continue Watching, Watchlist and Favorites. Random Movie and Random Show open details; Random Episode uses your usual source selection.
- My Channels: build a personal channel from movies and series, choose All Episodes, Next Unwatched or Latest Episodes, preview the schedule and adjust estimated runtimes. New channels keep story order by default.
- My Channels guides and playback share the same persistent schedule. Join the current programme, play from the beginning, or return to the live channel; refreshes preserve the programme already on air.
- Experimental playback options add selectable post-play recommendations, movie skip timestamps where available, and optional timed content warnings. Warning notices do not automatically skip or mute playback.
- Clear-art titles recover from oversized images and unsupported SVG artwork, including titles whose logo was previously missing.
- Improved connected-list browsing, detail presentation and expanded synopsis sheets, with updated French, German and Spanish interface coverage.
- Updated the playback engine and media libraries while retaining Cedar playback integrations.

## Critical changes

- Parental controls now cover additional direct, offline and custom-channel playback routes, source management and Multiview. Unrated, genre and Live TV restrictions are enforced more consistently.
- Delayed account errors from an old login or profile no longer clear a newer connection.
- Download retention and transfer rules follow the profile that owns each download. Interrupted downloads can recover; completed files from removed profiles remain manageable under Downloads / Removed Profiles.
- Recording failures are reported more accurately: missing segments are retried, empty or prematurely ended recordings are not reported as complete, and supported extensionless HLS streams are recognized.
- Explore continues through filtered pages and preserves the failed page for retry, reducing prematurely empty discovery results.
- Downloaded playback asks Resume or Start from Beginning before opening the player and uses Cedar's built-in player for private local files.
- Recording cancellation waits for the recording task to stop before removing its schedule and partial file; cancellation errors are visible.

## Experimental features

Sports Bar, My Channels, Quick Actions, and the new post-play, skip-timing and content-warning options are experimental. Coverage and behavior may vary by source, title and device; some things may still need adjusting.

Sports coverage, channel matches, skip markers and timed warnings depend on available data. Use your own authorized sources.

## Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV — parity in progress

- The public Android TV / Google TV / Android-based Fire TV release remains 1.2.85 (88). No new Android APK or OTA update is being announced with this roundup.
- Local parity candidates 89–91 brought progressive subtitle delivery and timing controls, the in-player guide, same-station alternatives, collection release ordering, offline changelog history, poster controls and connected Trakt/MDBList branches.
- Current development adds Cinematic and Detailed Home, an independent hero carousel, updated detail layouts and backdrops, primary/secondary hero sources, combination modes, title limits, metadata and ratings controls.
- Sports Bar is implemented in local Android development. Profile-copy fixes improve source and credential isolation, cancellation and rollback, and preserve saved Home branches.
- Phone/computer browser setup is published at the Cedar setup page for compatible development builds, including source entry and Home configuration. SVG title artwork now requests a compatible image rendition.
- My Channels and the remaining Home, poster, playback and cross-platform acceptance work are still being implemented or verified. Full Apple/Android parity is not complete. Physical-device and provider playback qualification remain pending.
- These development features are experimental and may still need adjusting. They are not a promise that every Apple feature is already available in the public Android build.

## Recent build history

- Apple builds 24–31: settings and source refinements, platform Home branches, widgets and sharing, connected lists, household tracking and new Apple TV Home choices.
- Apple builds 32–34: connected-list loading, Branch Group settings and Apple TV Home, carousel and focus refinements. Builds 32 and 33 were internal iPhone/iPad releases.
- Apple builds 35–38: unified posters, Favorites, Cedar Overlays, progress and completion controls. Builds 35–37 were internal iPhone/iPad releases; build 38 brought the accumulated changes to external testers.

- Apple build 41: coordinated hero artwork/metadata, earlier title-logo resolution and native iOS hardware-volume feedback. This is the previous external release on all Apple platforms.

- Android 1.2.85 (88): the last public Android release, with custom Home TV guides and bundled studio/streaming-service artwork. Later parity candidates and the current development work remain unreleased.
