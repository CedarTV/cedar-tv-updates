        READY FOR CEDAR

        Your Home, ready to take with you.

        Your Home is made only of Creator setups, so it can also travel as a link. On iPhone, iPad or Mac, Open in Cedar shows what will be added before anything changes. Apple TV receives the branches from your profile.

        Open in Cedar
        1. Download your locked .cedar profile file.
        2. In Cedar on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple TV, choose Settings → Profiles → Import Profile, or open the file.
        3. Enter your passphrase and choose what to bring in. Cedar fetches each Tobacco Tie and adds it to Cedar Library, or turns a catalog-only one into Branches.
        1. Download your .cedar file.
        2. Open it with Cedar on iPhone, iPad, or Mac, or choose Settings → Home Screen & Branches → Import Branches.
        3. Review the import in Cedar. Importing the same Home again updates those Branches instead of adding copies.

        Direct Branches import as poster rows. Branch Group device layouts carry over to each device. This preview does not send changes directly to a device.

        File type

        These are your own addresses and may contain your access token. They are only ever saved inside the locked file, on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
