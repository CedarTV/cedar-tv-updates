Privacy Policy

Cedar is local-first. Cedar requires no developer-operated user account and operates no advertising network, analytics backend, or media service. Optional sync and connected-service features are described below.

Data on your device

Profiles, library records, viewing progress, source configuration, downloads, and preferences are stored locally. Provider passwords, access tokens, and API keys are stored in the system Keychain rather than ordinary preferences. Removing a source or connected account removes Cedar’s stored credential reference. Deleting Cedar removes its app-container data; Keychain retention can also depend on operating-system behavior.

Optional Apple sync

If you enable iCloud sync, Cedar stores the profile, library, playback state, and preferences needed to keep your devices consistent in your private CloudKit container. A Cedar Household encrypts portable provider credentials on your device before placing them in a private CloudKit share. Invited household members can use supported portable values after accepting Apple’s invitation. Device-administration secrets stay local.

Services you connect

Cedar contacts only services needed for features you choose: configured media sources, metadata and artwork providers, optional tracking or watchlist accounts, subtitle or add-on endpoints, and public sports-data providers. Requests go directly from your device to those services and are governed by their privacy policies.

If you connect Trakt, Simkl, or MDBList, Cedar sends that service the connected account identifier and the title identifiers, progress, timestamps, history, watchlist changes, or ratings needed for the sync actions you enable.

Optional Cedar Link

Cedar Link bridges an Apple profile to linked Cedar devices and the browser companion through an end-to-end encrypted relay. The relay stores authenticated ciphertext plus random space and device identifiers, timestamps, authorization, and delivery state. It cannot read the full profile or provider credentials in plaintext. The browser’s derived companion key can manage presentation, Home shelves, linked devices, and playback controls, but cannot decrypt the full profile or provider credentials.

Cedar Link retention and deletion

The relay keeps encrypted journal records and limited routing metadata while a Cedar Link space is active. Revoking a device removes that installation’s relay authorization. To remove an active space’s relay records, delete its linked profile in Cedar before deleting the app. Copies already stored locally on another linked device remain until removed there.

YouTube trailers

Trailers play only after you choose one. On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, Cedar loads an embedded YouTube player; on Apple TV, it opens YouTube externally. The embedded player connects directly to Google/YouTube, may use cookies and collect device, network, and viewing information, and may show advertising under Google's policies. Google privacy policy.

Optional AI search

If you enable AI search with your own Google Gemini API key, Cedar sends the search text you submit directly to Google to generate recommendations. The key is stored in Keychain and is sent to Google to authenticate requests. Cedar does not append provider credentials or your viewing history to the search prompt. Google's retention and use of prompts and responses depend on your API service plan and region; unpaid services may use them to improve Google products. Do not include sensitive information in prompts. Gemini API terms.

Payments

The planned App Store launch is fully free, including cosmetic options, and offers no purchases. Earlier TestFlight previews may show unfinished Supporter purchase screens. Cedar does not collect payment card or bank details. Services you separately subscribe to remain subject to their own terms.

Diagnostics

Cedar does not automatically send diagnostics to a Cedar server. Optional reports are redacted, remain on the device, and leave it only when you explicitly export and share one. Apple may process App Store and device diagnostics according to your Apple privacy settings and Apple’s policies.

Tracking and advertising

Cedar operates no advertising network or developer analytics service and does not sell personal information or provide it to data brokers. Third-party services have their own data practices. In particular, the embedded YouTube player may use cookies, personalize content, and show advertising under Google's policies.

Permissions

On supported iPhone and iPad devices, camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a one-time Cedar device-link QR code; manual code entry is available. Cedar does not capture or upload photos or video. Local-network access is used to discover and connect to Cedar devices, remotes, DVRs, and media servers you configure.

Network security

Cedar prefers HTTPS. Some user-operated media servers support only HTTP, so Cedar can connect to an address you supply only after warning that cleartext connections can expose credentials, guide data, and viewing activity. Cedar refuses redirects from HTTPS to HTTP.

Children and content

Cedar is not directed to children under 13. The media visible in Cedar is determined by sources the user configures. Cedar provides no movies, shows, channels, playlists, add-ons, media-provider subscriptions, or provider credentials.

Your choices and contact

You can disconnect services, remove sources and profiles, disable optional sync features, revoke linked devices, export or delete local data, or delete the app. Cedar operates no user-account database, so there is no Cedar account to export or delete. Use the public support page for privacy questions and contact options.
