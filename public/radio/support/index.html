CEDAR RADIO FOR iPHONE & CARPLAY

Radio Support

Help with radio playback, Apple Music, CarPlay, and your station library.

A station will not play

  1. Try the station again after a few seconds; Cedar Radio reconnects automatically when a stream drops.
  2. Confirm the iPhone has an active Wi-Fi or cellular connection.
  3. Try another station. Independent broadcasters sometimes change or retire their stream address.
  4. If an imported station repeatedly fails, delete it from Library and import its newest playlist or direct stream URL.

Song details or artwork are missing

Cedar Radio first reads metadata supplied by the station, then attempts to match the artist and title against music catalogs. Some stations omit metadata, broadcast programme names instead of tracks, or use text that cannot be matched reliably. In those cases Cedar Radio keeps showing the station artwork and name.

Add to Apple Music is unavailable

  1. Wait for Cedar Radio to identify and match the current song.
  2. Tap the Apple Music control beside the main play button.
  3. Allow Media & Apple Music access when iOS asks.
  4. Confirm the device is signed into Apple Music and the song is available in the current storefront.

CarPlay

Connect the iPhone to a compatible vehicle or CarPlay receiver, open Cedar Radio, and choose a station under Favorites or Stations. Track title, artist, album, playback state, and available artwork are provided to the system Now Playing screen.

Managing your library

In Library, swipe right to favorite a station and swipe left to delete it. Your library and listening history are stored on the device and synchronize through your private iCloud account when available.

Collections and backups

Touch and hold a station to move it into a named collection. In Settings, Back Up Library creates a portable Cedar Radio document that can be saved in Files or iCloud Drive; Restore Library merges that backup with stations already on the device.

Station availability and rights reports

Some broadcasters do not allow playback in third-party apps. If Cedar Radio reports this restriction, use the broadcaster’s official app or website. For incorrect listings, objectionable content, or a rights concern, email support with the station name, stream URL, and a description. Rights holders can request a listing review or removal.

Privacy

Read Cedar Radio’s full privacy policy, including local storage, external station requests, MusicKit authorization, and deletion controls.

Still need help?

Email Cedar Radio support with the station name, what you expected to happen, and your iOS version. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
