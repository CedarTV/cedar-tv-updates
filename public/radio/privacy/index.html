CEDAR RADIO FOR iPHONE & CARPLAY

Radio Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026. This policy applies to the Cedar Radio app.

Our approach

Cedar Radio has no Cedar account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information. Broadcasters may include advertising in their programmes.

Information Cedar Radio stores

Stations, favorites, collections, Discover preferences, appearance settings, station health, and listening history are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. When iCloud is available, your stations, collections, favourites, and listening history synchronize through your private CloudKit database. Learned track corrections synchronize through iCloud key-value storage. The developer does not receive a copy.

You can delete stations with a left swipe in Library and remove listening records from History. Reset learned track details in Settings. Removing the app removes local data but may leave private iCloud data and backups. Manage those separately in your Apple Account’s iCloud settings and Files.

Library backups

If you choose Back Up Library, Cedar Radio creates a JSON document containing your saved stations, favorites, collections, and appearance settings. You choose where to save or share that document through Apple’s Files interface, including iCloud Drive. Cedar Radio does not receive a copy. Anyone with access to the backup can read the station information it contains.

Requests to other services

Station searches go to Radio Browser. Playing a station connects directly to the broadcaster. Station artwork can require requests to a station’s published website or image host. Track details may be sent to Apple’s music catalog, Apple’s iTunes Search API, and LRCLIB to locate matching songs, artwork, album information, and lyrics.

These services receive your IP address and normal network request information, and may retain requests under their own privacy policies. Cedar Radio does not operate these services. Radio Browser supplies directory information; it does not grant Cedar Radio rights to every stream or image it lists.

Apple Music

Cedar Radio requests Apple Music authorization only after you choose an Add to Apple Music action. MusicKit handles the authorization process. Cedar Radio does not receive your Apple ID password or payment information.

Tracking and analytics

Cedar Radio contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or account profile. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no data collection by Cedar Radio. It declares access to app-only preferences for appearance, Discover choices, and other settings.

Retention, deletion, and consent

Library and history records remain until you delete them; deletions synchronize through iCloud when available. Local artwork and lookup caches may be retained to reduce repeat requests. Backups you export remain until you delete them. You can revoke Apple Music access in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security. Cedar Radio does not retain a server-side copy that requires a separate deletion request.

Children

Cedar Radio is not designed for children. Radio programmes and lyrics are provided by third parties and may contain mature content.

Policy changes and questions

If Cedar Radio’s data practices change, this page and the App Store privacy information will be updated. For privacy questions, visit the Cedar Radio support page or email Tyler.bigwin@outlook.com.
