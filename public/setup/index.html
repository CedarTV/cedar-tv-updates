From here.

Open Quick Setup → Open Browser Setup on your Android TV, then scan its QR code. Edit this TV’s settings, add sources, and arrange Home from your phone or computer.

Requires an Android build with Browser Setup. Keep the TV on its setup screen. Scan within five minutes; connected access ends after 30 minutes or when you close setup. Setup data is kept in memory and is cleared when you leave this page.
