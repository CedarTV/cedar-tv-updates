Content Policy

Cedar is a player and organizer. It provides no movies, shows, channels, playlists, manifests, subscriptions, or provider credentials.

YouTube terms

Cedar’s embedded trailers use YouTube API Services. By using the embedded player, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy governs information received by the player.

Bring only authorized sources

Connect only media and services you own, operate, or are authorized to access. You are responsible for following the terms of every source and service you connect.

No source directory or resale

Cedar does not sell subscriptions, recommend unlicensed providers, scrape protected services, run a streaming proxy, support peer-to-peer delivery, or execute third-party plug-ins. Manifest and playlist responses are data from endpoints a user chooses.

Downloads

Cedar offers downloads only when a configured source provides a downloadable copy. The user is responsible for having permission to download and retain that media.

Metadata and trailers

This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. Metadata may also be provided by TheTVDB. Trailers play only after the user chooses one. On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, Cedar uses an embedded YouTube player governed by Google’s policies; Apple TV opens YouTube externally.

App Review demo

Cedar’s reviewer playlist contains Big Buck Bunny, an open movie released under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. It contains no password, subscription, customer data, or commercial provider source.

Rights concerns

Contact Cedar support with a specific title, source, and rights concern. Do not send provider credentials or private playlist addresses. Cedar can address its own materials but cannot control media supplied by an independent service.
