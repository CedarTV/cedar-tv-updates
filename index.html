What’s on

What Cedar does, laid out like its own TV guide. Pick a programme to read about it.

  1. Home

    Branches and the hero

    1. Branches, your way

      Home is built from branches you control. Add, rename, reorder, hide or restore them, and choose poster, wide or hero rows.

    2. The hero

      Choose the hero’s style and details, which rating sources it shows, and whether a trailer starts after a moment.

    3. Ready-made Homes

      Browse curated setups and branch groups in Cedar Creator, add a profile photo, and open them in Cedar.

      Open Cedar Creator

  2. Live TV

    Guide, catch-up, DVR

    1. The guide

      Playlist order, XMLTV programme data, a focused grid guide, and an in-guide preview player keep channels fast to browse.

    2. Catch-up

      Play programmes that have already aired, within the window your provider keeps an archive for.

    3. Multiview and DVR

      Jellyfin and Emby Live TV join Live TV on Apple devices, with lineups, multiview and Cedar DVR.

      Apple release notes

    4. Customize Live

      Arrange the Live page as shelves on iPhone, with programme pages that show where a show airs.

  3. Playback

    Picture and sound

    1. Your languages

      System-language audio, subtitles off by default, and preferred audio and subtitle languages, including forced and SDH tracks.

    2. Skip Intro and Up Next

      Skip intros where segment data exists, with an optional Up Next card and countdown before the next episode.

    3. Dolby Vision

      Optionally convert supported Dolby Vision Profile 7 material to Profile 8.1 as it plays.

  4. Discover

    Search and picks

    1. What Should I Watch

      Ranked picks in Search on Apple devices, which you can save as a Home row.

    2. Top 10 Charts

      Top 10 Charts and public MDBList lists, right on Home.

    3. Sports Bar

      Scores on Home, with as much detail as each competition’s data allows.

  5. Sources

    Servers and shares

    1. Plex, Emby and Jellyfin

      Connect your media servers and browse their movies, series, seasons and episodes with their own artwork.

    2. WebDAV shares

      Browse and play folders you’re authorized to use, with movie and series rows built from file names.

    3. Playlists and guides

      Add a remote M3U playlist and XMLTV guide, or pick local files on iPhone and iPad.

      Open browser setup

  6. Extras

    And the rest

    1. Cedar Radio

      Radio stations by country and genre, with synced lyrics and Shazam.

      About Cedar Radio

    2. .cedar files

      Share and import .cedar setup files, with passphrase-locked passwords.

      Open Cedar Creator

    3. Mark Segments

      Send intro, recap and credits times to IntroDB and SkipDB from Apple devices.

How to install on your TV
  1. On your TV, install the free Downloader app from the Amazon Appstore or Google Play.
  2. Allow Downloader to install apps. On Fire TV, open Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown apps. If Developer options is hidden, open About and select your device name seven times. On Google TV and Android TV, open Settings › Apps › Security & restrictions › Unknown sources.
  3. Open Downloader, enter cedartv.github.io/cedar-tv-updates, and choose Download for Android TV.
  4. Select Install, then open Cedar. Future updates are offered inside the app.

Already installed? Use Browser setup to add sources and arrange Home from your phone or computer.
