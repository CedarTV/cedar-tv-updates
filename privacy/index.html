Privacy Policy

Cedar is local-first. Cedar requires no developer-operated user account and operates no advertising network, analytics backend, or media service. Optional sync and connected-service features are described below.

Data on your device

Profiles, library records, viewing progress, source configuration, downloads, and preferences are stored locally. Provider passwords, access tokens, and API keys are stored in the system Keychain rather than ordinary preferences. Removing a source or connected account removes Cedar’s stored credential reference. Deleting Cedar removes its app-container data; Keychain retention can also depend on operating-system behavior.

Optional Apple sync

If you enable iCloud sync, Cedar stores the profile, library, playback state, and preferences needed to keep your devices consistent in your private CloudKit container. A Cedar Household encrypts portable provider credentials on your device before placing them in a private CloudKit share. Invited household members can use supported portable values after accepting Apple’s invitation. Device-administration secrets stay local.

Services you connect

Cedar contacts only services needed for features you choose: configured media sources, metadata and artwork providers, optional tracking or watchlist accounts, subtitle or add-on endpoints, and public sports-data providers. Requests go directly from your device to those services and are governed by their privacy policies.

If you connect Trakt, Simkl, or MDBList, Cedar sends that service the connected account identifier and the title identifiers, progress, timestamps, history, watchlist changes, or ratings needed for the sync actions you enable.

If you link Discord and turn on Share What I’m Watching on iPhone, iPad or Mac, your device sends Discord the status your profile allows while Cedar plays: the title, episode, a public poster image and the elapsed time, or only that you are watching something in Cedar. It is sent straight from your device to Discord through Discord’s SDK, clears when playback stops, and is never available to Kids profiles. Your Discord sign-in is kept in the Keychain and syncs to your other Apple devices through iCloud Keychain; it is never added to a Cedar Household. Review the Discord privacy policy.

If you also turn on Share Ratings, the rating sheet offers to post your rating in the #ratings channel of Cedar’s Discord server. Only when you share, your device sends Cedar’s feedback service the title’s TMDB or IMDb identifier, the season and episode number, your score and your Discord sign-in. The service uses the sign-in once to look up your Discord account and confirm you are in the server, and does not keep it. It takes the title, year and poster from TMDB and posts them with your score, mentioning your Discord account, for everyone in the server to see. So that rating the same title again updates the same post, it keeps your Discord user ID, the title, your score and the post’s ID for up to a year. Removing the rating in Cedar deletes the post and that record. Share Ratings is off by default and is not offered to Kids profiles.

Cedar Link (retired)

Cedar Link, the optional encrypted relay that bridged an Apple profile to other Cedar devices and a browser, has been retired. Updated Cedar apps remove leftover Cedar Link sign-ins from the device. Until the relay shuts down, it keeps only the encrypted records and limited routing metadata it already held, which it cannot read.

YouTube trailers

These consent controls are being prepared for the App Store release; earlier TestFlight builds may not include them yet.

On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, selected trailers use YouTube’s embedded player. Google receives identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, viewing and advertising interactions, and technical usage data for playback, personalization, analytics, and advertising. The App Store launch implementation asks before loading each session, uses temporary web storage, and disables autoplay. iPhone and iPad also require Apple tracking permission; if declined, the user can open YouTube externally. Embedded YouTube is unavailable in Kids profiles. Closing the player ends the local session but does not delete information already held by Google. Apple TV opens YouTube externally. Using the player is subject to YouTube’s terms: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms and Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Optional AI search

AI Search uses your own Gemini API key. Google receives the key, your network information, and descriptions you submit; Cedar does not append media-source credentials or viewing history. Google currently retains prompts and responses for 55 days for abuse monitoring. Unpaid requests may receive human review and support product or model improvement. Paid service data terms differ. Do not enter sensitive information. The App Store launch implementation requires a device-local privacy choice for each profile before validation or search, an adult acknowledgement, and confirmation of Google’s regional and billing requirements. Kids profiles cannot use AI Search. Turn off data sharing and save in Gemini settings, or remove the connection, to stop future requests. These acknowledgements do not verify age, location, or billing. Gemini terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms ; retention: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/usage-policies ; supported regions: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/available-regions

Payments

The planned App Store launch is fully free, including cosmetic options, and offers no purchases. Earlier TestFlight previews may show unfinished Supporter purchase screens. Cedar does not collect payment card or bank details. Services you separately subscribe to remain subject to their own terms.

Feedback and bug reports

When you send a bug report or feature request from the Cedar website or app, the Cedar bot posts it publicly in the Cedar Discord’s bug or feature-request forum, without your name: your summary and description, the app or device you chose and, if you include it, the Cedar version. The app’s system version and device model, and anything you write in the staff-only box are never posted; they’re kept only with the private report on Cedar’s feedback service on Cloudflare. Reports are read by the Cedar developer and never sold or used for advertising. Reports from older app versions aren’t posted automatically.

If you attach diagnostics in the app’s Send Feedback, the app’s redacted diagnostics export is uploaded as a file to a private staff channel in the Cedar Discord, which only the Cedar team can see, and your private report links to it. It stays there until staff delete it.

The report form in the Cedar Discord posts your title, device, Cedar version and description publicly in the server’s bug or feature-request forum, under your Discord name, and sends the same report to Cedar’s feedback service. Anything you write in its staff-only box is never posted; it’s kept only with the private report. That form’s daily limit is counted with a salted one-way hash of your Discord user ID that changes every day. When you report in the Cedar Discord, the Cedar bot sends you a direct message when staff pick up your report and when its status changes. If you choose Stop these updates, Cedar keeps your Discord user ID so it doesn’t message you again; choosing Turn updates back on removes it.

The website and current app versions don’t ask for an email address. If an older app version sent one, it’s never posted, is kept only with the private report, and is deleted as soon as the report is reviewed. A reviewed report that isn’t kept with a private work ticket is deleted after 30 days; one that is is kept privately for the Cedar developer while that ticket exists, and deleted within 30 days of the ticket being removed. If your report has a post in the Cedar Discord, later replies in that post are copied to the same private ticket. Unreviewed reports are deleted after a year. A daily abuse counter uses a salted one-way hash of your network address that changes every day; the address itself is not stored. If the Cedar developer gives a report a public post in the Cedar Discord, the post is written fresh, without your report, name or email.

Diagnostics

Cedar does not automatically upload native app diagnostics to a Cedar server. Optional local reports leave the device only when you export and share them, or attach them to Send Feedback in the app, which sends them only to Cedar staff. Google services can retain performance and technical diagnostic information associated with their requests for operation, security, and analytics. Apple diagnostics follow your Apple settings and policies.

Tracking and advertising

Cedar operates no advertising network and does not sell personal information. The embedded YouTube service can use identifiers, approximate location, and video or advertising interactions across apps and websites for advertising and measurement. Cedar therefore discloses third-party tracking. The App Store launch version loads the player only after session consent and, on iPhone and iPad, Apple tracking authorization. Gemini search text is disclosed for functionality, analytics, and other purposes such as unpaid model development, not as advertising data.

Permissions

On supported iPhone and iPad devices, camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a one-time Cedar device-link QR code; manual code entry is available. Cedar does not capture or upload photos or video. Local-network access is used to discover and connect to Cedar devices, remotes, DVRs, and media servers you configure. On Apple TV, Cinema Lighting asks for Home access to dim and restore the lights in the room you choose, and, only if you choose a sunset schedule, for location access to look up the Apple TV’s approximate location once. The location and your HomeKit choices stay on that Apple TV and are never sent to Cedar.

Network security

Cedar prefers HTTPS. Some user-operated media servers support only HTTP, so Cedar can connect to an address you supply only after warning that cleartext connections can expose credentials, guide data, and viewing activity. Cedar refuses redirects from HTTPS to HTTP.

Children and content

Cedar is not directed to children under 13. The media visible in Cedar is determined by sources the user configures. Cedar provides no movies, shows, channels, playlists, add-ons, media-provider subscriptions, or provider credentials.

Your choices and contact

You can disconnect services, remove sources and profiles, disable optional sync features, revoke linked devices, export or delete local data, or delete the app. Cedar operates no user-account database, so there is no Cedar account to export or delete. To take down a rating you shared to Cedar Discord, remove the rating in Cedar or ask through the support page. Use the public support page for privacy questions and contact options.
