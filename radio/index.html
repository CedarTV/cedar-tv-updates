CEDAR RADIO FOR iPHONE & CARPLAY

Live radio, beautifully tuned.

Cedar Radio brings station discovery, a personal library, song details, and CarPlay to iPhone.

Your stations, together

Search the worldwide Radio Browser directory, browse by country, or import your own authorized station URLs and playlists. Save favourites and organize collections.

Know what is playing

View song details, artwork, history, and lyrics when available. Add matched songs to your Apple Music library.

Listening on the go

Use background playback, lock-screen controls, AirPlay, and CarPlay. Your private library can sync through iCloud.

Support and privacy

Cedar Radio is being prepared for App Store release. Visit Radio support or read the Radio privacy policy. Content availability depends on broadcaster terms and regional availability.

Cedar

A focused media player and organizer for the screens you already own. Connect only sources and services you are authorized to use.
