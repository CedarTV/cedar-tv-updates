## 5. Website Attribution Guidelines

When showcasing these configurations on your website:
1. **Always show the creator's name** clearly on the setup card (e.g., `by SkyNet`, `by Kaptain`, `by callandt95`).
2. **Provide a link back to the source** where applicable (e.g. `get-stranded.fabriceleven.com/p/{id}`, `kit.betterer.cc`).
3. **Retain metadata fields** in the exported `.json` and `.strand` files so downstream users know who authored the setup.
4. **Distinguish Sourced vs. Unsourced** configs for users wanting pre-filled catalogues vs. customized metadata keys.
