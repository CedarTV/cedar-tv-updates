Android TV changelog

Cedar for Android TV release notes.

Current and historical releases for Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV, with their matching build numbers and notes.

New Cedar app for Android TV

Early beta, not a finished release.

Cedar for Android TV is an early beta. Some features from the Apple apps are still missing, and you may run into bugs. Please report them so they can be fixed.

The new Cedar app installs alongside the original Cedar app. Existing users of the new app, including Cedar Preview installs, can update from Settings → Software Update. The original Cedar stable release remains available below.

Cedar Android TV 1.0.0 · build 87

Quick Actions filtering, Playlist Cleaner, more list links, Branch design overlays, Restore to Continue Watching, Merge Duplicate Files and a Box Score team switcher.

Early beta Version 1.0.0 Build 87 November 25, 2026
Download Cedar 1.0.0 (build 87) GitHub release notes Visual release notes Browser setup

November 25, 2026 · Early beta for Android TV and Google TV.

Highlights

  • Quick Actions filtering — choose a genre and then a decade inside a Quick Actions tile before it launches, for Random actions or every action.
  • Playlist Cleaner — hide Live TV categories you never use and tidy channel names with the new Cleaner in Live TV & DVR settings.
  • More list links — paste Trakt, Letterboxd, boxd.it, TMDB collection, IMDb chart or public MDBList links to add them as Branches.

Home and Branches

  • Movies and TV Shows Branches — the Movies and TV Shows pages leave out Live TV, sports, radio, recordings and Quick Actions Branches.
  • Branch designs — Logo and Marquee designs gain overlays, and Marquee no longer rotates on its own. VHS and Spine Numbers are retired.
  • Random picks never come up empty — Random Movie and Random Show fall back to popular TMDB titles when your libraries have nothing to offer.
  • Restore to Continue Watching — bring back titles you removed from Continue Watching.

Live TV, sports and calendar

  • Box Score team switcher — switch teams with their logos in Box Score, with columns that fit the screen or scroll.

Playback and sources

  • Merge Duplicate Files — identical files offered by several sources merge into one choice; on by default and can be turned off in Source Preferences.

Fixes

  • Random Episode no longer picks Live TV channels.
  • Tobacco Tie cached flags and runtime labels are read correctly.
  • Viewing Stats shows posters correctly again.

Known issues

  • DTS:X and DTS Express aren’t decoded yet, and TrueHD Atmos plays as 7.1.
  • A new app icon appears after you leave Cedar.
  • Subtitle translation on Apple TV and Android TV needs your own Gemini key, and never runs on Kids profiles.

Cedar Android TV 1.0.0 · build 86

Movies and TV Shows pages, public TMDB/Letterboxd links and MDBList charts, Sports Bar arrangement, Tobacco Tie live channels, Addic7ed subtitles, skip chips, Jellyfin/Emby/Plex Resume and FFmpeg 9.0.2.

Early beta Version 1.0.0 Build 86 November 23, 2026
Download Cedar 1.0.0 (build 86) GitHub release notes Visual release notes Browser setup

November 23, 2026 · Early beta for Android TV and Google TV.

Highlights

  • Movies and TV Shows pages — give Movies and TV Shows their own hero, Branches, layout and IPTV categories, configured per profile in Settings › Home & Branches.
  • TMDB, Letterboxd and MDBList charts — paste any public TMDB or Letterboxd list link to add it as a Branch without an account, or choose from twelve built-in MDBList charts.
  • Sports Bar arrangement — reorder sports, pin favourite leagues to the top and hide leagues you never watch, saved with your profile on every device. Requested by dr.aaboulhosn on Discord.
  • Live channels from Tobacco Ties — import 24/7 channels from Tobacco Ties straight into Live TV alongside your playlists, with live, upcoming and replay status badges.

Home and Branches

  • My Teams 7-day schedule — My Teams Home Branches now show your teams’ upcoming games for the next seven days alongside live games and today’s results.

Discovery, ratings and stats

  • Redesigned person pages — actor and creator pages feature a hero backdrop, multi-role summaries, photo galleries and cleaner filmography grouping.
  • AniList ratings — anime titles can display AniList ratings alongside your other rating sources. Requested by RyZ3n on Discord.
  • Viewing Stats from watch history — Viewing Stats caches past plays from connected Trakt, Simkl and MDBList accounts for fuller history and insights. Requested by RyZ3n on Discord.
  • Bilingual and Arabic title matching — bilingual provider titles and Arabic numerals match TMDB entries more reliably without losing diacritics or subtitles.

Live TV and sports

  • Missing guide labels — replace empty No Event Scheduled guide slots with colour-coded blocks titled Channel Name, Live Broadcast or Local Programming.
  • Sports matchup artwork — Sports Bar and Sports Branch cards render full team matchup artwork matching Apple TV.
  • M3U playlist accuracy — repeated channels across groups keep separate entries, display names come from the playlist title, and Xtream live, movie and series paths are respected.

Playback, subtitles and servers

  • Addic7ed TV subtitles — search TV episode subtitles from Addic7ed with no API key required, toggled under Online Subtitles.
  • Next-episode source matching — auto-playing the next episode stays close to the resolution, HDR, release group and audio language you were already watching, and reads structured AIOStreams metadata.
  • Skip chips and media server Resume — one-shot Intro, Recap and Preview skip chips and direct Resume and Next Up syncing from Jellyfin, Emby and Plex arrive from Apple TV.
  • Post-play recommendations — after a film or series ends, Watchlist and catalog recommendations rotate per title instead of repeating the same four cards.
  • Player engine and FFmpeg 9.0.2 — Apple devices update AetherEngine to 7.72.0 with hardware bwdif deinterlacing, inverse telecine and MPEG-TS Dolby Vision; Android TV updates its audio decoder to FFmpeg 9.0.2.

Profiles and parental controls

  • Regional parental ratings — Kids rating limits compare titles against your region’s own certification system rather than relying only on US ratings.
  • Discord community welcome — a one-time Home prompt after installing or updating invites adult profiles to join the Cedar Discord community.

Fixes

  • Creating a Home Branch no longer carries over state from previously opened premade options.
  • Short-lived dropped live streams back off cleanly instead of triggering rapid reconnect loops.

Known issues

  • DTS:X and DTS Express aren’t decoded yet, and TrueHD Atmos plays as 7.1.
  • A new app icon appears after you leave Cedar.
  • Subtitle translation on Apple TV and Android TV needs your own Gemini key, and never runs on Kids profiles.

Cedar Android TV 1.0.0 · build 85

Subtitle translation, six new languages, the full Sports Bar with cricket and Pick a Channel, synced Radio lyrics, release labels on posters and Kids Rating Limits that hide titles.

Early beta Version 1.0.0 Build 85 November 20, 2026
Download Cedar 1.0.0 (build 85) GitHub release notes Visual release notes Browser setup

November 20, 2026 · Early beta for Android TV and Google TV.

Highlights

  • Subtitle translation — translate subtitles into the language you choose, set for each profile. Show Original Too keeps the original line above the translation.
  • Six new languages — Cedar is now in Italian, Japanese, Polish, Russian, Turkish and Spanish for Spain, alongside Latin American Spanish.
  • Release labels — posters can show Release Date, Return Date, Airing, Renewed, Series Cancelled or Binge Ready at a glance.
  • Right Now Branch — add a Home Branch with live games, favourite channels on now and reminders starting soon.
  • App icons — every app icon is free for everyone, and all eight, Film Strip included, are on Apple TV and Android TV.

Home and Branches

  • Server collections — add a Jellyfin or Emby collections view as a Branch Group, with one Branch per collection.
  • Branch names — Branch names Cedar writes for you follow the app language.

Posters and appearance

  • Rank Bookmark — ranked titles can show their rank as a bookmark tucked into the poster’s corner.
  • Poster colour — a reworked colour band, new Color Only and Tinted Minimal styles, and ratings in the genre line.
  • Appearance & General — settings are regrouped into Theme, Focus, App Icon & Extras and General cards.
  • Film Strip — Film Strip Dark is retired, and anyone using it moves to Film Strip, art by armadillo.

Playback and subtitles

  • Align with Embedded — corrects subtitles that slowly drift out of time, not just a fixed delay.
  • Subtitles follow the profile — subtitle timing fixes, the sync offset and Translate belong to each profile.
  • Playback Buffer — choose how far ahead Cedar reads a film or episode: 5 minutes, 15 minutes or as much as fits.
  • Server Order — rank your media servers so Cedar tries your preferred server first.
  • IMDb episode ratings — episodes can show IMDb ratings, with your own OMDb key, in Advanced Metadata.
  • Trakt reviews — title pages can show Trakt community reviews, without connecting Trakt.
  • Next episodes — Cedar learns more reliably when you like the next episode to start.

Live TV and sports

  • Cricket — follow international cricket, the IPL, the Big Bash League and the Sheffield Shield, with scorecards.
  • Pick a Channel — link a game, team or league to the right channel, or mark one Not This Channel.
  • Game window — Back shrinks a playing game into a window while you read its game page.
  • List layout — show the Sports Bar as a compact list of games, league by league.
  • Sports filter bar — chips for Following, All Sports and each sport playing today sit under the page title.
  • Club names — games match channels even when your provider uses a club’s local-language name.
  • Sports Bar on Android TV — search, Hide While Live scores, More Games and tennis, golf and Formula 1 views arrive from Apple TV.

Radio

  • Synced lyrics — Radio shows time-synced lyrics for the song playing, as on Apple TV.
  • Lyric sync — lyrics highlight only when Cedar knows the song’s place. Select a line to sync them.

Settings and profiles

  • Source Filters — filter the source picker from its top bar, as on Apple TV.
  • Tobacco Ties — turn a Tobacco Tie’s parts on or off and change its address without reinstalling it.
  • Profile order and trailer language — reorder profiles and choose the language for trailers.
  • Rapid Mode and Simkl sign-in — Limit Rapid Mode Switching works, and Simkl signs in with a browser code.

Fixes

  • A PIN check that can’t read the PIN says so instead of reporting a wrong PIN.
  • Kids profiles hide titles above the Rating Limit from Home rows, Search and Explore.
  • Search no longer shows results for a query you were still typing.
  • A saved stream link that stops working recovers instead of failing playback.
  • Imported DVR settings keep their excluded sources.

Known issues

  • DTS:X and DTS Express aren’t decoded yet, and TrueHD Atmos plays as 7.1.
  • A new app icon appears after you leave Cedar.
  • Subtitle translation on Apple TV and Android TV needs your own Gemini key, and never runs on Kids profiles.

Cedar Android TV 1.0.0 · build 84

Radio, DTS and TrueHD audio with Downmix, large files that keep playing, a rebuilt Live TV with Right Now, Kids Screen Time and Film Strip icons.

Early beta Version 1.0.0 Build 84 November 15, 2026
Download Cedar 1.0.0 (build 84) GitHub release notes Visual release notes Browser setup

November 15, 2026 · Early beta for Android TV and Google TV.

Highlights

  • Film Strip icons — two new app icons with art by armadillo from Cedar Discord, in light and dark. Choose them in Settings › App Icon.
  • Branch Group folders — pick a group’s folders, add more later, start from tiles, filter every folder at once, and copy Branches to other folders.
  • Stats for Nerds — redesigned with chips, five columns and three live graphs for the stream you’re watching.
  • Radio — listen to radio stations on your TV and keep your favourites, as on Apple TV.
  • DTS and TrueHD audio — these soundtracks play on any TV or soundbar, with a Downmix setting for stereo and 5.1 speakers.

Home and Branches

  • Folder Branch editor — a folder Branch opens straight away, with Choose Content and poster previews.
  • Remove Empty Folders — tidy a Branch Group in one step by removing folders with nothing in them.
  • Navigation editor — the editor for Cedar’s pages has been redesigned.
  • Poster Play/Pause — choose whether Play/Pause on a poster plays the title or opens its Branch.
  • Kids Screen Time and Rating Limit — set Screen Time and a Rating Limit for each Kids profile.
  • Import a Profile — bring a profile from a .cedar file onto your TV.
  • More from Apple TV — Cedar Lists, Year in Review, the Minimalist look, a Screensaver, Poster Studio and Pop-out Top 10 arrive from Apple TV.

Live TV and sports

  • Reorder custom channels — put your custom channels in the order you want.
  • Show in Categories — list a Custom Lineup alongside the Live categories.
  • Multiview from Tobacco Ties — add a Tobacco Tie live stream to Multiview.
  • Fixtures order — league fixtures follow the league’s order, with the home side first for soccer and similar sports.
  • Fight posters — feature the current bout, and title fights get a gold Title Fight label.
  • Fight results — Hide While Live keeps bouts that are already decided on the card.
  • Live TV — rebuilt around Right Now, with what’s on across your channels.
  • Sports on Home — sports Branches on Home, with fight cards for fight nights.

Playback and sources

  • Subtitles above the controls — subtitles move up while the player controls are showing, so the controls never cover them.
  • Emby Connect — sign in to Emby with your Emby Connect account.
  • Live reconnects — a live stream that plays cleanly for a minute gets its full reconnect allowance back.
  • Arabic search — search and title matching treat Arabic spelling variants as the same, such as أحمد and احمد.
  • Large files — very large files keep playing: a stalled connection reconnects instead of buffering forever.
  • Large playlists — huge Xtream playlists import in about a minute instead of several.
  • Profile loading — profiles load and switch faster, most noticeably on Fire TV Stick.

Fixes

  • Huge playlists no longer stop with “catalog exceeded its safety limit”.
  • Continue Watching offers the next episode without a tracking service, and Trakt no longer asks to reconnect.
  • Poster overlays draw at full size, focus rings are no longer dimmed, and Back works in the guide.

Known issues

  • Kids Rating Limit blocks titles above the limit when they’re played, but doesn’t hide them from Home rows, Search or Explore yet.
  • DTS:X and DTS Express aren’t decoded yet, and TrueHD Atmos plays as 7.1.
  • Search is no longer on the Live page. Use the main Search to find channels.
  • Search rebuilds its index after the update, so results may be incomplete for a few minutes.
  • A new app icon appears after you leave Cedar.

Cedar Android TV 1.0.0 · build 12

A fresh early beta line with a new look that matches Apple TV, Viewing Stats, Live TV Branches, Follow people and When Resuming.

Early beta Version 1.0.0 Build 12 November 4, 2026
Download Cedar 1.0.0 GitHub release notes Browser setup

November 4, 2026 · Early beta for Android TV and Google TV.

Cedar for Android TV starts a fresh beta line at 1.0.0. It replaces 1.4.1, brings Android much closer to Apple TV, and reworks the look throughout.

Highlights

  • New look — Settings, Explore, Library, Sources and detail pages are redesigned to match Apple TV.
  • Viewing Stats — see your watching in numbers. Experimental.
  • Live TV Branches — put live channels and what is on now on Home.
  • Follow people — follow actors and directors, and their work gathers in Your People folders on Home.
  • When Resuming — choose Ask, Always Resume or Always Start Over. Always Resume is the default. Requested by pH_low on Discord.

Home and Branches

  • Branch Shuffle — shuffle a Branch, then Shuffle Again for a new order. Requested by JamminJerome on Discord.
  • Branch Titles — show titles on two lines, one line, or hide them.
  • Branch import review — check Branches before they are added from a file.
  • Empty Branches — Branches that load nothing are left out of folder pages.
  • Drop Show — drop a series from Next Up cards, whichever tracker you use.
  • Only Show Titles I Can Play — Search and Explore leave out titles your playlists and libraries don't have.
  • Search People — actors and directors appear in search results.

Posters and appearance

  • Theme styles — Cyber and Halloween.
  • Country flags and sport icons — show or hide them on live channel names.
  • Spectrum badges — Cedar's built-in quality badges for the player.
  • Landscape artwork — each card loads one image, so Branches fill in faster.
  • Ratings — scores of 0.0 are hidden.

Playback

  • Arrange player controls — put each player control in the top bar, bottom bar or options, or hide it.
  • Picture adjustments — brightness, contrast, saturation and gamma, with presets such as Cinema, Sports and Anime. Experimental. Requested by lionel on Discord.
  • Reuse Last Link — play the stream that worked last time for a title.
  • Refresh Progress — turn off the progress refresh pop-up.

Sources and Live TV

  • IPTV playlist icons — playlists show their own icon, and titles show Playlist Availability badges.
  • Sports channel matching — the guide matches sports channels more accurately.
  • Long seasons — seasons with more than 150 episodes are split into ranges of 100.
  • MDBList calendars — calendar lists load, and Out Today uses the release date.

Feedback

  • Send Feedback — post to the public tracker from Settings, with optional private diagnostics.
  • Requested — see the titles you have requested.

Fixes

  • Holding Select no longer triggers the wrong action.
  • Up Next no longer jumps to the next episode early when an episode has wrong credits timing. Requested by Citharae on Discord.
  • Episodes that are only on your media server are no longer hidden when TMDB lists fewer.
  • Signing in to Jellyfin or Emby on one profile no longer signs out another.

Not yet on Android

  • Cedar Tracking hub, Multiview layouts, Top 10 numbering and Match Logo colour.
  • Sports Branches in the shared editor, and hiding sports scores.
  • Stalker serial and device IDs, and the simplified poster menu.
  • Search Subtitles, and Infuse or external players for media server streams.
  • Surround Sound output.

Update from Settings → Software Update, or install the APK over your current Cedar app from this release line. The original Cedar Android app (version 1.2.85) uses a separate package and does not receive this update.

  • Receives end-to-end encrypted profile updates through Cedar's ciphertext-only relay while keeping the television's last working local profile available during network failures.
  • Supports bounded cross-platform compression for mature Apple profiles that exceed the original sync-document size, without increasing the relay's public request limits.
  • Keeps small profile documents backward compatible and rejects oversized or malformed compressed payloads before import.
  • Improves pairing, linked-profile status, manual resync, and failure messaging throughout Android TV settings.
