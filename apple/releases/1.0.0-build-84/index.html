Version 1.0.0 Build 84

Build 84 brings Film Strip, two community-made app icons with art by armadillo. Branch Group folders are easier to build: pick folders from a group, start from tiles and copy Branches between folders. Stats for Nerds has been redesigned with live graphs, and subtitles now lift above the player controls. On Android TV, Radio arrives, DTS and TrueHD audio play with a Downmix setting, and very large files keep playing instead of stalling.

5

Fixes

15 fixed

  • Home no longer stays on Loading Home… with a large Xtream playlist and Only Show Titles I Can Play. Apple devices
  • Media server search covers the whole library, and a large import no longer holds up detail pages. Apple devices
  • Large Plex imports retry and resume where they stopped. Apple devices
  • Silo plays Dolby Vision Profile 7 originals, and seeking in remuxed files lands in the right place. Apple devices
  • Simkl numbers anime episodes correctly, and Trakt renews its sign-in before it expires. Apple devices
  • Dexter: Resurrection opens the right show, and titles match by their original title too. Apple devices
  • TMDB air dates no longer show a day early or late. Apple devices
  • Switching profile from the Apple TV sidebar asks for the PIN. Apple TV
  • The search field keeps its text after Back, and tapping Search again focuses it. iPhone
  • A grey oval no longer appears in the middle of the player. iPhone, iPad
  • Trailers no longer crash on Mac, and opening in Infuse no longer crashes on iPhone. iPhone, Mac
  • Branch captions no longer leave a gap when a title has no year. Apple devices
  • Huge playlists no longer stop with “catalog exceeded its safety limit”. Android TV
  • Continue Watching offers the next episode without a tracking service, and Trakt no longer asks to reconnect. Android TV
  • Poster overlays draw at full size, focus rings are no longer dimmed, and Back works in the guide. Android TV
6

Known issues

6 known issues

  • Kids Rating Limit blocks titles above the limit when they’re played, but doesn’t hide them from Home rows, Search or Explore yet. Android TV
  • DTS:X and DTS Express aren’t decoded yet, and TrueHD Atmos plays as 7.1. Android TV
  • Search is no longer on the Live page. Use the main Search to find channels. Android TV
  • Search rebuilds its index after the update, so results may be incomplete for a few minutes. Android TV
  • A new app icon appears after you leave Cedar. Android TV
  • Film Strip icons come in a later build. Until then, App Icon is hidden in Settings. Apple TV
