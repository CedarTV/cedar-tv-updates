Version 1.0.0 Build 79

IPTV movies and series get their own Movies and TV Shows pages, and streaming service folders now fill themselves with Top 10, New on and Coming Soon Branches. A built-in VPN carries movies and shows while live channels stay direct. Sports opens like the rest of Cedar, with tennis draws, golf leaderboards and F1 weekends. The player gains Arrange Controls, Picture adjustments and Surround Sound, and Airplane Mode turns your downloads into a full-screen Ready to Watch view.

Highlights

4 new, 2 improved

New

Movies and TV Shows pages

IPTV movies and series get their own pages, with Branches on top and category boxes with counts below. Rename, hide, rearrange or add any box to Home.

Improved

Streaming service folders

Single-service folders gain Top 10, New on, Coming Soon, Originals and Hidden Gems Branches. Existing groups offer to add them once.

New

VPN

Built-in WireGuard VPN with sign-in for popular providers or your own config. Movies and shows use it while live channels stay direct, with per-source choices.

Improved

Sports pages

Games and teams open like posters, and the player’s sports button shows a row of game cards with your teams first.

New

Airplane Mode

With no network, Cedar shows your downloads in a full-screen Ready to Watch view.

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Mac
New

Guide history

Press Left on a programme to walk back through earlier programmes on any channel. Back returns to the logo and jumps to Now.

Home and Branches

4 new, 2 improved

Shuffle

New

A Shuffle sort for any Branch, including Top 10 and charts, with Shuffle Again.

Requested by JamminJerome on Discord

Live TV Branches

New

Add IPTV categories, favourite or recent channels, custom channels and custom lineups to Home as Branches.

Requested by flyboy787 on Discord

Follow people

New

Follow actors and directors from their page or a cast portrait, and build Branches from their work.

Enhanced Posters

Improved

Band Color matches a fog band to the artwork, plus Score Color, new labels such as In Cinemas, and starter styles.

Titles

Improved

Edit Branch has one Titles picker: Two Lines, One Line or Hide.

Folder focus art

New

Folders that carry focus art switch to it when highlighted.

3

Playback

5 new, 2 improved

Arrange Controls

New

Put each player control in the top bar, bottom bar or Options menu, or hide it. In Settings › Playback › Player Controls.

Picture (Experimental)

New

Brightness, contrast, saturation and gamma, with presets such as Cinema, Vivid and Anime, from the player’s Options. SDR video only.

Requested by lionel on Discord

Surround Sound

New

Convert DTS and TrueHD audio to Dolby Digital Plus or lossless PCM for your receiver. In Settings › Audio & Subtitles.

Reuse Last Link

New

Replays the last working stream for a title without searching again. In Source Settings › Memory.

Play Using

Improved

Native Player and installed players such as Infuse and VLC now appear in Play Using.

  • Apple TV

Cedar volume

New

The player has its own volume and mute, separate from the Mac’s, with ⌘↑, ⌘↓ and M.

  • Mac

Subtitle preview

Improved

Plays a real clip, matches the player’s sizes and applies changes straight away.

Fixes

13 fixed

  • Playback no longer refreshes Home and the title page behind the player.
  • A playback error keeps the controls and offers Choose Another Source.
  • Long source lists no longer make the player stutter.
  • Xtream movie and series lists import in seconds instead of minutes.
  • IPTV episodes no longer leave a second Continue Watching card that won’t open.
  • AIOStreams bridge servers find sources again, and identical Tobacco Tie streams show once.
  • “No Tobacco Ties Installed” no longer shows after switching profiles.
  • Series missing from TMDB, or with extra provider episodes, list all their episodes.
  • Creating a Cedar Household no longer crashes.
  • Full Screen no longer closes a game started from Home. iPhone, iPad
  • Choose Source in sports plays Tobacco Tie streams directly. Apple TV
  • Large and wide posters no longer overlap when focused, and wide Branches no longer stop half off screen. Apple TV
  • Choose Source in sports opens the full player. Mac

Known issues

2 known issues

  • Silo can be added under Media Servers, but its copies can’t play yet.
  • Watch Together (SharePlay) appears in the player options but is not ready yet.
