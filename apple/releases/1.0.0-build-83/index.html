Version 1.0.0 Build 83

Build 83 is the first Cedar build with one number on every platform: iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac and Android TV. Source Cards puts card layout, fields and tags on one page with a live preview. Jellyfin and Emby collections can become Branches, the source picker can filter its results, and SMB playback no longer stalls. On Android TV, Branch Groups arrive with every Apple TV preset, along with Top 10, For You, Upcoming and Home DVR Branches, Sleep Timer and Mark Segments.

Highlights

5 new

New

Source Cards

One page for card layout, fields and tags, with a preview of your last source search. New fields show video codec, bitrate, Dub · Sub and subtitles.

New

Server Collections

Jellyfin and Emby collections, including lists your server’s plugins sync, become Branches in the server’s order. Open a server’s page and choose Branches.

  • Apple devices
New

Source picker filters

Set Picker Top Bar to Filters for chips that narrow one picker without changing your saved rules.

  • Apple devices
New

Branch Groups

Every Branch Group preset from Apple TV, from streaming services, networks and studios to genres, decades, sagas, directors and actors, plus Cross With… to combine two folders.

  • Android TV
New

More Branches

Top 10 Chart, Top Picks for You, Because You Watched, Upcoming, and Home DVR Branches for Recordings, Recording Now and Recorded Shows.

  • Android TV

4

Fixes

7 fixed

  • SMB playback no longer stalls partway through large files. Apple devices
  • AV1 files whose container reports the wrong bit depth now play. Apple devices
  • Xtream playlists that send TMDB IDs as tmdb_id show Playlist Availability badges after their next refresh.
  • Game cards show AET and PEN for football, and OT and SO for other sports.
  • On tvOS 26, the detail page’s Play button and info chips no longer look black. Apple TV
  • Swiping to delete a Branch in Home & Branches no longer closes Settings. iPhone, iPad
  • Continue Watching and Next Up use the same order. Android TV
5

Known issues

2 known issues

  • Titles from SMB shares don’t open in Infuse or other external players yet. Play them in Cedar. Apple devices
  • Server Collections Branches, source picker filters and the redesigned Tobacco Tie settings aren’t on Android TV yet. Android TV
