Get Cedar

Your media. Your devices. Your control.

A calm home for the media you already have.

Cedar brings authorized media sources, live channels, personal libraries, and watch progress into one private, local-first player for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Mac.

Local-first by design

In the app

Made for each screen.

Screens from Cedar itself, on each device it’s made for. The titles, artwork and channels are Cedar’s own made-up demo catalogue.

1

Bring authorized sources

Connect media servers, playlists, and services you own, operate, or have permission to use. Cedar ships with no media, subscriptions, or provider credentials.

2

Keep credentials protected

Provider passwords, tokens, and API keys are kept in the system Keychain rather than ordinary preferences. Cedar has no required developer-operated user account.

3

Choose what syncs

Use Cedar entirely on-device, or opt into private Apple sync. Cedar Household adds an optional encrypted way to share selected profile data.

4

No tracking or ads

Cedar has no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated analytics backend. Services you deliberately connect receive only the requests needed for the features you enable.

Straightforward policies

Cedar supplies the player, not the content.

Connect only sources and services you are authorized to use. The public privacy, content, and accessibility pages explain how the app behaves before you install it.
